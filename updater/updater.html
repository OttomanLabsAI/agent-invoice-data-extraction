How it works

      
The app lives in the app folder next to updater.py. An update downloads the chosen version from GitHub, unpacks it, moves your data folder (settings, Gmail token, invoices) and the app's installed parts across, then deletes the old version and the zip. Close the app's window before updating. Start the app runs run.bat (Windows) or run.sh (Mac) for you; the app then keeps running on its own.
